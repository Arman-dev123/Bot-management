How This Works

1
Configure in NICE CXone

Create a Live Chat channel in ACD, assign a Studio script with your bot, and copy the widget script.

2
Paste credentials above

Enter the Script URL, Brand ID, and Channel ID from your NICE CXone Website Script page.

3
Click "Load Widget"

The NICE DFO loader module is injected and cxone('init', ...) / cxone('chat', 'init', ...) are queued and run.

4
Chat with the Bot

Click the launcher to open the NICE DFO Live Chat panel. Messages route through your Studio script to the Dialogflow bot.
